Beta · work in progress

Still being built

Worldfall is in beta: an unfinished version that keeps getting updates with new creatures, places and things to do. Everything on this site is how it looks today. Expect bugs and rough edges, and expect it to keep changing.

All assets are subject to change: models, pictures, sounds and features may look and work differently in later updates.

Added lately

  • The map and the 3D world keep the same night: lit houses, smoke and doors on both
  • People really live inside: at the table, in bed, by the hearth, and they use the doors near you
  • Look inside: roofs fade under the mouse, and G takes people over where they are
  • Ask "I'm looking for someone" or read the town hall's ledger to find anyone
  • Forging with quality, lights you set down, and kings' orders for ships and guards
  • Far trees and buildings as 3D models on the graphics card

Worldfall is in beta: a work in progress that keeps getting updates, and all assets are subject to change. It is an unofficial fan mod for WorldBox, which is made by Maxim Karpenko. Every picture on this site was captured in the game with the mod running.

Headings set in Alagard by Hewett Tsoi.
